
Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

IPTV - Arty ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data may be collected when you use the App, how it is used, and what choices you have regarding your data.
1. Information We Collect
User-Provided Playlist Data

The App allows users to add their own M3U playlist URLs, Xtream Codes credentials, or direct stream links in order to access media streams provided by third-party sources.

Playlist credentials are primarily stored locally on your device.

We do not intentionally transmit playlist credentials to our servers unless required for optional cloud synchronization or when you explicitly submit diagnostic information.
Analytics Data (Android app)

On Android, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage information such as app launches, feature usage, navigation patterns, and session stability.

This information does not directly identify individual users and is used only to improve app performance and reliability.
Error Reports

Users may optionally send manual support or error reports.

These reports may include:

device model

operating system version

technical logs

user-provided issue descriptions

Sensitive playlist credentials are removed or masked before transmission whenever possible.
Advertising Data (Android app)

On Android, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect identifiers such as:

Android Advertising ID

These identifiers may be used for fraud prevention, ad delivery, and ad personalization depending on your device settings and consent preferences.
Cloud Synchronization (Android app)

If cloud features are enabled in the Android app, selected app data such as favorites, settings, and playlist metadata may be securely stored using Supabase infrastructure.
2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

provide and maintain App functionality

improve performance and stability

analyze crashes and technical issues

support optional cloud synchronization

display advertisements where applicable (Android app)

We do not sell personal information.
3. Data Storage and Retention

Most user data remains stored locally on your device.

If cloud synchronization is used in the Android app:

synchronized data is retained only as long as needed to provide the service

users may remove stored data by clearing app data or contacting support

We do not monitor, inspect, or control the content of user-provided playlists or media servers.
4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

request access to your personal data

request correction or deletion of stored data

withdraw consent for analytics or advertising identifiers (Android app)
5. Third-Party Services

The Android app may rely on:

Google Play Services

AdMob

Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics

Supabase

The iOS app relies only on Firebase Crashlytics (see section 8).

Each provider maintains its own privacy policy.

8. iOS App (iPhone and iPad)

This section describes the iOS app "Arty Player" (bundle ID com.arty.iptv) for iPhone and iPad. The iOS app does not use Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, or Supabase. The sections above that describe those services apply to the Android app.

What the iOS app does not do

It has no accounts and no sign-in.

It does not send your playlists, credentials, favorites, history or settings to any server operated by us.

It does not track you and does not show advertising.

It does not use any advertising identifier, including Apple's IDFA.

It does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's Application Support folder and UserDefaults:

playlists you add, including M3U playlist URLs and Xtream Codes logins

favorites

viewing history

settings

the electronic program guide (EPG) cache

Xtream Codes login details are stored on your device. They are sent only to the server you entered for that login.

Network connections

The app connects only to:

the playlist, Xtream Codes, EPG and stream URLs that you enter. These are often plain HTTP addresses, because many IPTV servers do not use HTTPS.

Firebase, to send crash reports (see below).

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash reporting service provided by Google, to receive crash reports. Crashlytics collects:

crash reports and diagnostics, including stack traces

device model

operating system version

app version

an installation identifier

Purpose: app stability. We use these reports to find and fix crashes. Crash reports are not linked to your identity, and they are not used for tracking, analytics or advertising.

Retention: Crashlytics keeps crash data according to Firebase's default settings, which is up to 90 days. For how Google handles this data, see Google's Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Deleting your data

Settings → "Reset All Data" deletes all of the app's data stored on your device.

Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

For questions about crash reports that have already been sent, contact us at artokn7@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

The iOS app is not directed to children under 13. Because the app has no accounts and does not collect personal information for identification, it does not ask children for personal details.

Support

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

Arty Player is a media player for M3U playlists and Xtream Codes accounts you already have access to. It ships no channels or content.

Contact

For help, email artokn7@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version and app version.

How do I add a playlist?

On iOS, go to Settings → Playlists → + and choose "M3U URL" or "Xtream Codes". On Android, open the Playlists tab and tap Add.

Do you sell or provide channels?

No. You need your own playlist from your service provider.

Why does my stream not play?

Check the URL in a browser or VLC, and check your subscription. You can also try the other player engine: on iOS, go to Settings → Player engine and choose Auto, Native or VLC. Plain http:// links are supported.

Why do some characters look wrong?

The app detects UTF-8 and older Turkish, Cyrillic and Western encodings automatically. If characters still look wrong, ask your provider for a UTF-8 playlist.

My provider rejects the app.

On iOS, go to Settings → User-Agent and try the other option.

How do I delete my data?

On iOS, go to Settings → "Reset All Data", or uninstall the app. Everything is stored on the device.
